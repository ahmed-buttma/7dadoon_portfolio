About Me

I'm Ahmed, a Software Engineering student at Bethlehem University. I love mixing logic and creativity: I write code, train dabke groups, and tell stories through a camera lens.

I've been a dabke dancer for 12 years, and for the last year I've been training students at Ghirass Center. I also work as an accountant for two fuel stations in Bethlehem.

I started as a wedding photographer for 3 years, then moved into freelance photography, ad editing and personal creative work such as films and storytelling photography. My film Freedom Is Close Yet Distant was selected at the CARLOTA International Film Festival in Vienna. On the software side, I've competed in programming contests across Bethlehem and Palestine and joined several hackathons.
