About Me

I was a wedding photographer for 3 years, then moved to freelance work: photography, ad editing. i also do personal creative projects such as films and storytelling photography.

My photography has been part of the Amman Photo Festival 5 times, and I also took part in the Al-Maamoura Film Festival in Abu Dhabi.

My film Freedom Is Close Yet Distant was selected at the CARLOTA International Film Festival in Vienna and nominated for the Palestinian Voice Award at the Political Film Festival in Istanbul, Turkey.
