Ulfa – Premarital Counseling App

App · Decision Tree · PMRS

Video

About

Built with the Palestine Medical Relief Society. The user picks their gender, then answers a chain of questions where each next question depends on the previous answer (a decision-tree approach). At the end, the app gives feedback about the user's personality.
